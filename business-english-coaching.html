Frequently asked questions

Questions before you start.

Is this suitable for advanced professionals?

Yes. Coaching can focus on high-level communication, precision, tone, natural phrasing, leadership language and the small errors that affect credibility.

Can we use my real work materials?

Yes. Where appropriate, sessions can use your presentations, meeting scenarios, interview questions or communication challenges as practical coaching material.

Do you teach teams as well as individuals?

Yes. Individual Business English coaching is available here, while companies and small teams can use the Group English Coaching programme.

Is coaching online?

Yes. Sessions are delivered online by Zoom, so professionals can join from the UK or internationally.
