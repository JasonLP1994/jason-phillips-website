How it works

Personalised from the beginning.

1. Define the outcome. We establish exactly what you need English to help you do.

2. Diagnose the gaps. I identify the language, pronunciation, fluency or communication issues holding you back.

3. Build the programme. Lessons and practice are tailored around your level, industry, timeline and goals.

4. Apply it. We practise the situations that actually matter: meetings, interviews, exams, presentations or real conversation.
