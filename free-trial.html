A few things you might be wondering

Is the trial really free?

Yes. Your first 25 minute trial lesson is free, with no payment details required and no obligation to book more. It’s available once per new student.

What should I bring?

Just an idea of what you want help with. If you have a presentation, interview question or English mistake you keep making, we can start there.

How do we arrange the lesson?

Choose an available time in the calendar above. Cal.com will confirm the booking and Zoom details automatically. If none of the available times work, use the alternative-time form and I’ll reply by email.

What happens afterwards?

We’ll talk about what would help you next. If you’d like to continue, paid coaching starts at £45 for a 50 minute lesson. Paid sessions have a separate 10 minute buffer between appointments.
