About Jason

A teaching career built across countries, classrooms and businesses.

I’ve spent more than 13 years teaching English, developing teachers and building learning environments where people can genuinely progress.

The story

Teacher. Leader. Founder.

My career has taken me from England to Thailand and Vietnam, working with young learners, adults, professionals and senior executives.

In Vietnam, I moved beyond my own classroom into teacher leadership and development. As Head Teacher at APAX English in Hanoi, I led and mentored around 15 native English teachers.

I later co-founded Star Language in Da Nang, growing the centre from zero to more than 150 students. I was involved across curriculum, recruitment, teacher training, marketing and day-to-day operations.

After roughly 12 years living in Asia, I returned to the UK to continue developing professionally and work towards PGCE and QTS.
