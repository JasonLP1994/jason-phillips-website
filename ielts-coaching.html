Frequently asked questions

Questions before you start.

Can you help with both Academic and General IELTS?

Yes. The coaching is tailored to your IELTS route and the specific skills you need to improve.

No tutor can responsibly guarantee an exam result. I focus on improving the language, strategy and performance factors within your control.

Yes. Coaching can concentrate on one skill or combine several depending on your priorities.

Will I get feedback on mistakes?

Yes. Detailed correction and practical feedback are central to the coaching so you know what to improve between sessions.
