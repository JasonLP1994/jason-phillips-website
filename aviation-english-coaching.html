Frequently asked questions

Questions before you start.

Do you teach ICAO English?

I can support aviation-related English, interview communication, fluency, pronunciation and scenario practice. If you require an official ICAO language proficiency test, you should use an approved testing provider for certification.

Can you run mock airline interviews?

Yes. We can practise realistic interview questions, scenario responses and follow-up questions, then refine your language and delivery.

Can cabin crew use this coaching?

Yes. Coaching can support cabin crew recruitment, English assessments, passenger communication and interview preparation.

Is this coaching online?

Yes. Sessions are delivered live online, making them suitable for aviation professionals in different countries and time zones.
