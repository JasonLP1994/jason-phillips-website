Group coaching FAQ

Questions about online group English coaching.

How many learners can join?

Private small groups are designed for 2–4 learners. Professional team coaching is typically suited to 4–6 people, depending on the communication goal.

Can you train a company team?

Yes. Team coaching can focus on meetings, presentations, client communication, negotiation, professional vocabulary, pronunciation and confidence.

Do group members need the same English level?

A similar level helps, but the most important factor is having a shared goal. I recommend a format after learning more about the group.

Is group coaching online?

Yes. Sessions are delivered live on Zoom, so groups can join from the UK or internationally.
