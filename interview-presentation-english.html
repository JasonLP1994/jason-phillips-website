Frequently asked questions

Questions before you start.

Can we do a mock interview?

Yes. We can rehearse realistic questions, analyse your answers and improve content, grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation and delivery.

Can you help with a presentation I already have?

Yes. We can work directly with your presentation, refine the language and practise how you deliver it.

How many sessions do I need?

It depends on the deadline and your current level. A single session can solve a focused problem, while the three-session Performance Intensive is designed for high-stakes preparation.

Can you help with confidence as well as language?

Yes. Confidence improves when your language, structure and rehearsal are stronger, so the coaching addresses both performance and English.
