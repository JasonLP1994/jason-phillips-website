Focused coaching

English built around the situations you actually face.

Frequently asked questions

Questions before you start.

Do I need to lose my accent?

No. The goal is clear, confident communication, not erasing your identity or forcing you to sound like someone else.

Can you teach British pronunciation?

Yes. As a British English coach, I can help you understand and practise British pronunciation patterns while keeping the focus on clarity and natural speech.

Will we only study sounds?

No. Pronunciation works best alongside vocabulary, sentence stress, connected speech, listening and real conversation.

Is this suitable for intermediate learners?

Yes. Coaching can be adapted from intermediate through advanced levels depending on your needs.
