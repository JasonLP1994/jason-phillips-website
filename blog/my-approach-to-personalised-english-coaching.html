Why confidence matters as much as grammar

After more than 13 years teaching English, I have noticed that capable people often struggle to express their ideas as confidently in English as they do in their first language.

By Jason Phillips · 21 October 2026 · 3 min read

One thing I've noticed after more than 13 years of teaching English is that some incredibly capable people struggle to express themselves as confidently in English as they do in their native language.

And that's something I've spent much of my career helping people overcome.

I've taught English in the UK, Thailand and Vietnam, built and successfully ran my own English language centre, and worked with more than 1,000 professionals from different industries and backgrounds.

Over the years, I've helped people prepare for important job interviews, improve their presentations, communicate more confidently in meetings, prepare for English examinations and develop the language skills they need to progress in their careers.

But something I've learnt is that improving your English isn't always about learning more grammar or memorising hundreds of new words.

Sometimes it's about learning how to use what you already know more naturally and confidently.

That's why I take a personalised approach to teaching. Everyone has different strengths, weaknesses, goals and reasons for wanting to improve.

Through Phillips English, I offer private online English coaching built around the individual, whether that's improving everyday communication, preparing for an interview or becoming more confident in a professional environment.

If you feel your English doesn't quite reflect your knowledge, experience or personality, feel free to get in touch. I'd be happy to have a chat.
