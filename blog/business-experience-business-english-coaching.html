The experience behind Phillips English.

How running a business shapes the way I work with professionals.

By Jason Phillips · 2 October 2026 · 2 min read

I want to share a bit more about the experience behind Phillips English.

Alongside 13+ years teaching in England, Thailand and Vietnam, I was cofounder and CEO of a successful, highly profitable language centre in Da Nang.

Running a business meant managing people, explaining decisions, handling difficult conversations and working across cultures. Clear communication mattered every day.

That experience shapes how I work with professionals now.

You might know your job inside out, but still struggle to explain your ideas in an English meeting or answer an unexpected question during a presentation. I understand how frustrating that can be.

At Phillips English, we prepare for those moments together. We work on your meetings, presentations, interviews and negotiations, including the wording, pronunciation and delivery.

You bring the situation. We practise it, work through what’s difficult and build your confidence with feedback you can actually use.

If you’re a professional, manager or business owner who wants to feel more comfortable communicating in English, send me a message.
