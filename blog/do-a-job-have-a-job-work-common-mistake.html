“My brother do a job” — why it should be “My brother has a job” or “My brother works.”

This is the kind of mistake that looks small but makes a big difference to natural communication. English learners often know the words job, work and do, but combine them in a way native speakers normally would not.

By Jason Phillips · British English Coach · Published 29 September 2026 · 6 min read
Instead ofMy brother do a job.
SayMy brother has a job. / My brother works.
Nailing the basics matters.
Fluent English is not only about advanced vocabulary. It is also about using very common words — such as job, work, have and do — in the combinations English speakers naturally expect.

The mistake: “My brother do a job”

A learner recently wanted to explain that his brother was employed and said:

✗ My brother do a job.

✓ My brother has a job.

✓ My brother works.

There are actually two issues in the original sentence. With my brother, the present simple form would be does, not do. But even “My brother does a job” would usually not be the natural sentence if your meaning is simply that he is employed.

Use “have a job” when you mean employment

If you want to say that someone is employed, have a job is the normal expression.

Have a job

Focus: the person is employed.

My brother has a job in finance.

Work

Focus: what the person does or where they are employed.

My brother works in finance.

Both are natural, but they answer slightly different questions.

  • Does your brother have a job? — Yes, he has a job.
  • What does your brother do? — He works in finance.
  • Where does your brother work? — He works for a bank.

So when can we say “do a job”?

Do a job is absolutely possible in English, but it normally refers to performing or completing a particular task — not simply being employed.

The plumber did a great job.

I need someone to do this job properly.

She did the job in less than an hour.

Here, job means a specific piece of work or task. That is different from saying someone has a job, where job means employment.

Job and work are not used in exactly the same way

This is another common source of mistakes. Job is usually a countable noun. Work is usually uncountable when we mean the activity of working.

✓ I have a job.

✓ I have a lot of work today.

✗ I have a work.

✗ I have many works today.

You can have one job, two jobs or several jobs. But when work means tasks or professional activity, we normally do not count it that way.

Common mistakes with the same idea

✗ My brother do a job.

✓ My brother has a job. / My brother works.

✗ I am work in a company.

✓ I work for a company.

✗ She has a work in London.

✓ She has a job in London.

✗ What is your work?

✓ What do you do? / What do you do for work?

Why these simple combinations matter for fluency

Learners sometimes chase difficult vocabulary because they think that is what makes English sound advanced. But natural communication depends heavily on collocations — words that commonly go together.

Have a job, go to work, work for a company, work as a teacher and do a good job are useful chunks. When you learn them as complete expressions, you do not have to build every sentence word by word.

That makes your speaking quicker, more accurate and more natural.

Useful patterns to remember

  • have a job — I have a job in marketing.
  • work in + industry/field — I work in technology.
  • work for + company — I work for an international company.
  • work as + role — I work as an engineer.
  • go to work — I go to work at eight.
  • do a good/great/bad job — You did a great job on the presentation.

Quick practice

Choose the most natural option.

  1. My sister does a job / has a job at a hospital.
  2. I work / am work in software development.
  3. He works as / works like an accountant.
  4. The designer did a great job / had a great job on the new website.

Answers: has a job · work · works as · did a great job

The takeaway

If you mean that someone is employed, say “He has a job” or simply “He works.” If you want to explain the profession, use work as, work in or work for.

Use do a job when you are talking about carrying out a particular task.

It is basic English, but that is exactly the point. The more automatic the basics become, the more fluent your communication becomes.
