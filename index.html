Online English coaching

Clear goals. Practical coaching. Measurable progress.

Phillips English provides private online British English coaching for learners who want their English to work in real situations. Professional clients can develop the language of meetings, presentations, negotiations, interviews and international communication. IELTS candidates can work on speaking, writing, fluency, vocabulary, grammar and exam strategy with focused feedback.

Coaching can also target pronunciation, natural spoken English, confidence, aviation English and specialist communication. Instead of following a generic course, sessions are shaped around your goals, current level and recurring mistakes, so the language you practise is useful outside the lesson.
