## What the data actually shows
- **31** published blog article files reviewed. **22** had recorded Google Search impressions; **9** had none in this export.
- Those 22 articles collectively had **805 impressions and zero recorded Google clicks**. The overall site received **1,176 impressions and 13 clicks**; the blog figures are a subset.
- The five highest-impression blog pages received **467 of the 805 observed blog impressions**. All recorded no clicks, but averages from this sample are too small to prove a specific ranking or conversion problem.
- **Caution:** These are historic observations **before** the latest SEO overhaul and before these changes. Average position is not a fixed rank, and no-impression pages may simply be new, niche or undiscovered in this period.

## Strategy and reasoning
1. **Protect existing visible content:** Preserve the recent topical updates on “before submitting”, “one of my favourite songs” and ED endings; avoid mass rewriting well-aligned pages.
2. **Answer the searcher's actual question:** The export shows queries such as *i agree or i am agree*, *before submitting*, *lead vs led*, *presentation in english*, *english for meetings*, *in on at*, *formal email phrases* and *what is better*. Updated descriptions, headings and worked examples use the relevant ordinary language of those topics.
3. **Fix a discovery gap:** The In/On/At article was included in the sitemap but missing from the main blog card grid; its card is now restored. Improve blog card labels for key weaker pages.
4. **Add original examples, not keyword stuffing:** Expand the practical teaching value of 12 pages with targeted sample sentences, use cases, corrections or speaking practice. Link naturally between useful articles and relevant coaching services.
5. **Protect conversions and navigation:** Preserve the existing build-time prominent 25-minute free-trial banner, Cal.com booking settings, package pricing, CSS and analytics.

## Follow-up measurement
Wait for Google's re-crawl and compare **impressions, clicks, CTR, and queries at the exact URL level** after approximately four weeks, using comparable date windows. Record confirmed free-trial enquiries separately; SEO ranking gains and bookings are not guaranteed. New articles planned for 23, 25, and 27 November are not part of this 31-article historical report.
