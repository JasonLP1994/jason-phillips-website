Is this for you?

I work best with people who want to use their English, not just study it.

  • You understand English but don't always sound as confident as you feel.
  • You need English for meetings, presentations, interviews or international work.
  • You want clear correction rather than someone simply keeping the conversation going.
  • You value lessons built around your weaknesses, interests and goals.
  • You want to see measurable progress and know exactly what to work on next.
